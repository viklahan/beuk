# Beuk — Roadmap

Where everything stands, sorted by status. Each item sits in exactly one
column and moves along as the work moves. Dates are the day a feature
reached the build; the [changelog](/changelog) explains the changes in
plain words.

Last updated: 2026-10-31.

**The statuses.** *In progress*: being built right now. *Planned*: agreed,
and in the order it will be built. *Exploring*: wanted, not yet designed
or not yet scheduled, and may change shape. *Shipped*: in the studio
today.

## In progress

- **Research files: web pages and Word files.** The Notebook already keeps
  pictures, PDFs, recordings, video clips and text on a note. Next it
  takes a web page you saved from your browser and a Word document.

## Planned

In the order they will be built.

- **Side-by-side documents.** Two parts of the book, or a chapter and a
  research file, open next to each other.
- **Scrivenings.** Several sections read and edited as one continuous
  page, each still its own section.
- **Track changes.** Edits shown as suggestions you accept or reject,
  one by one or all at once.
- **Billing and legal.** Pricing decided and checkout switched on, with
  the legal pages finalised alongside it. Until then nothing can be
  bought.
- **Launch on beuk.studio.** The production server set up by the launch
  checklist (server, firewall, DNS and mail records, a first backup and
  restore drill), then a smoke test after every deploy.
- **Bring your own AI key.** Connect your own AI key, built on the
  Covenant's per-entry AI setting (stored in the book today, hidden until
  this lands).
- **Typography themes per account.** Your own typesetting themes, kept
  with your account and used across books.
- **Series tombstone.** A server-side record that a series was left or
  renamed, so a second device's unsent edit can never bring it back.

## Exploring

- **Co-authoring with AI.** Writing alongside an AI that knows your
  Covenant and your Notebook, with every suggestion yours to take or
  leave. Needs the AI key above first.
- **Beuk Weekly, every week.** The School's newspaper (issue No. 1 is
  out) published weekly, with news, articles, author notices, contests
  and things to do around the site. Drafted with help; a person reads
  and approves every issue, and nothing goes out without that.
- **Community, on its triggers rather than a calendar.** At around
  100–500 writers: a Made-with-Beuk gallery, shareable typesetting
  templates, one seasonal publishing challenge a quarter, a monthly
  author spotlight, and a weekly hosted writing sprint. Once 50 or more
  writers have committed and moderation time is set aside: a real
  community room, self-hosted, starting with one room. Email stays the
  official support channel. NaNoWriMo's 2025 closure is the standing
  warning that moderation failure is existential, so this waits for its
  trigger.

## Shipped

Newest first.

- **2026-10-31 — Room for research.** A book now keeps up to 100 MB of
  research files, 25 MB each (it was 25 MB, 10 MB each). Pictures and
  files are saved on your device once instead of with every keystroke's
  save, so a book full of them types as quickly as an empty one.
- **2026-10-29 — Research files.** Pictures, PDFs, recordings, video
  clips and text kept on a Notebook note, opened beside it, with a link
  to where they came from.
- **2026-10-29 — Custom fields.** Your own text, list, checkbox and date
  fields on Outline cards, list rows and Grid columns.
- **2026-10-29 — The Notebook.** Research, ideas and scraps kept with the
  book, in folders, searchable, pinned to chapters, and aware of the
  Covenant's names.
- **2026-10-29 — Compare.** A changed section shown word by word against
  its History snapshot.
- **2026-10-29 — The Outline Grid and plot threads.** Sections against
  Covenant entries, filled in from the text, plus your own thread
  columns with a note per section.
- **2026-10-29 — RTF import.** Manuscripts from Word, Scrivener,
  TextEdit and LibreOffice saved as RTF.
- **2026-10-29 — Caife and Homeport.** A thank-you paper boat on the
  support page, and an anchor in the studio's top bar that opens the
  home page.
- **2026-10-28 — The Covenant grows up.** Changes pinned to the story and
  shown "right now", links, quick facts, genre templates, marking a
  change from the desk, and one shared Covenant per series for
  signed-in writers.
- **2026-10-28 — Beuk School and Beuk Weekly No. 1.** The Maiden Voyage
  course with signed-in progress, and the School's newspaper.
- **2026-10-27 — Print bleed, box sets and long-book PDF/X.** Box sets
  keep every book whole; print PDF/X works for long books; full screen
  for every reader.
- **2026-10-26 — Accounts, the whole site and Help.** Your account page,
  a front door, a searchable Help room, smaller History and truer
  counts, safer chapter moves, and Story Insights you can explore.
- **2026-10-24 — Saved on this device, and a Hardcover preview.** Your
  book is saved in your browser as you write, and the preview shows the
  printed hardback.
- **2026-10-22 — The studio, whole.** The writing and typesetting studio
  itself.
- **Before launch — the studio wired to its server.** Books sync to your
  shelf, with email confirmation, password reset and sign-in from more
  than one device.
- **Before launch — the build, M1–M5.** Accounts and books on your shelf
  (M1), the export archive and billing hooks (M2), the Housekeeping
  admin surface (M3: audit rows, one lock, scrubbed outputs, the
  Self-checks registry run server-side, backup and restore rehearsal,
  gated promotion), Warmth Phase 0 (M4: the Help reading room and EPUB
  error decoder, a named human support email, the changelog, this
  roadmap, the status line), and M5: the full official EPUBCheck suite
  run server-side and true PDF/X-1a conversion for print interiors.
