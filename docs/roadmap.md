# Beuk SaaS — Roadmap

Rendered from this file by Housekeeping's Reference pillar and by the
public roadmap page. The full plan lives in the launch workspace
(`phase-two-saas-plan.md`); this is the repo's own copy, kept in the
Now / Next / Later shape the public page shows.

## Now

- **Shipped in the build (M1–M5).** Accounts and books on your shelf
  (M1), the export archive and billing hooks (M2), the Housekeeping
  admin surface (M3: audit rows, one lock, scrubbed outputs, the
  Self-checks registry executing server-side, backup/restore
  rehearsal, gated promotion), Warmth Phase 0 (M4: the Help reading
  room and EPUB-error decoder, a named human support email, the
  changelog, this roadmap, the status line, the streak forgiveness
  audit), and M5: the full official EPUBCheck suite run server-side and
  true PDF/X-1a conversion for print interiors, long books included.
- **The studio wired to this API.** Books sync to your shelf, with
  email confirmation, password reset and sign-in from more than one
  device. The [changelog](/changelog) lists everything since, in the
  order writers met it.
- **The Covenant, grown up.** Changes pinned to the story and shown "right
  now", links, quick facts and genre templates, marking a change from the
  desk, and one shared Covenant per series for signed-in writers.

## Next

- **Billing and legal.** Pricing decided and checkout switched on
  (until then the billing routes answer "billing not configured" and
  nothing can be bought), and the legal pages finalised alongside it.
- **Launch on beuk.studio.** The production server set up by the
  launch checklist (server, firewall, DNS and mail records, first
  backup and restore drill), then the smoke test after every deploy.
- **BYOK AI.** Connect your own AI key, built on the Covenant's per-entry
  AI setting (stored today, hidden until then).
- **Per-user Typography themes.**
- **Series tombstone.** A server-side record that a left or renamed series
  is gone, so a second device's unsent edit can never recreate it.

## Later — warmth on its triggers, not on a calendar

- **Phase 1 (~100–500 users):** Made-with-Beuk gallery, shareable
  typesetting templates/themes, one seasonal publish-path challenge
  per quarter, a monthly author spotlight, a weekly hosted sprint run
  as an event rather than a permanent server.
- **Phase 2 (50+ writers pre-committed + moderation time
  ring-fenced):** a real community — Discourse self-hosted on the same
  Hetzner system, one room to start, seeded with 20+ founder threads.
  Email remains the official support channel. (NaNoWriMo's 2025
  closure is the standing warning: moderation failure is existential,
  so this phase waits for its trigger.)
