SWOP2006_Coated3v2.icc — attribution and terms of use
=====================================================

The PDF/X job embeds this CMYK output profile as the DestOutputProfile
of every converted interior's GTS_PDFX OutputIntent. The profile is NOT
stored in this repository: deploy/Dockerfile.tools downloads it at
image build time from the ICC Profile Registry and checks its hash.

  ICC profile ID (MD5 per ICC.1, computed over the downloaded file):
           f7b89b0445878f17ebb85d7515da9a98 — equals the "Profile ID" the
           registry entry lists.

           Grade 3 coated publication paper"
