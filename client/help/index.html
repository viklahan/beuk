The Founders' Shelf

Beuk is opening its first seats to a small shelf of founding writers — the people whose books and grumbles will shape what gets built next. There's no public community (yet, deliberately: a quiet room with twenty writers beats an empty hall), but the list is open. Leave your name and a line about what you write, and the invitation will come to you.
