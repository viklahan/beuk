Write

A writing desk with the book beside it

Chapters on the left, the page you are writing in the middle, and the book as a reader will see it on the right. Bring a Word, Markdown, plain-text, RTF or PDF manuscript, or start from an empty desk.

The preview turns pages on a Kindle Paperwhite, Kindle Oasis, Kobo Clara, Kobo Forma, an iPad in Apple Books, an iPhone, an Android tablet and more, and tells you roughly how many pages the book makes on that screen.

  • Word / Markdown / text / RTF / PDF import
  • Footnotes and pictures
  • Drop caps and small-caps lead-ins
  • Device preview

Typeset

The Typography Studio

Fifteen full-book themes across fiction, nonfiction, memoir, academic, children's and poetry shelves. A theme sets the fonts, the rhythm of the lines, the paragraph style and the ornaments together, and every value stays yours to adjust.

Use the studio's own book fonts or upload a font you have the rights to embed. Write in Paper by day or Candlelight at night.

  • 15 themes
  • Your own fonts
  • Scene-break ornaments
  • Paper and Candlelight

Print

Print interiors, spread by spread

The same manuscript set as a paperback or hardback interior in 24 trim sizes, with mirrored margins for the binding, running heads and page numbers. Turn through it spread by spread before you print.

Choose whether chapters open on a right-hand page, and make a Large Print edition from the same text. On your device, the PDF is made with your browser's own Print → Save as PDF; with an account, Beuk's server converts the print interior to PDF/X-1a.

  • 24 trim sizes
  • Chapters open on a recto
  • Large Print edition
  • Margin guides on screen only

Cover

The cover maker and the print wrap

Seven layouts, backgrounds from a plain colour to your own artwork, and every text block on the cover can be dragged exactly where you want it.

The print wrap puts back, spine and front in one file at the size print-on-demand printers ask for. The spine is worked out from the page count and the paper, and you can download the wrap as a PDF or a JPG, or turn the finished book around in 3D.

  • Seven layouts
  • Your own art
  • Spine from page count and paper
  • Wrap PDF / JPG

Check & send

Check it, then export it for each store

Eleven EPUB presets: Amazon KDP, Apple Books, Kobo, Nook, Draft2Digital, OverDrive, Smashwords, Tolino, Scribd, Google Play and a generic EPUB. Each rebuilds the same book with what that store expects.

Store-ready? gives one pass/fail report against KDP's or IngramSpark's own published print rules, and nothing is marked as passed unless it was actually checked. The checks that read the server-made PDF/X file, such as embedded fonts, need an account; without one they show as not checked. The package inspector shows exactly which files go into your EPUB.

The same dialog also exports Markdown, HTML and DOCX, a box set that combines several books in one EPUB, and narrator scripts for an ACX audiobook.

  • 11 store presets
  • Store-ready? report
  • Markdown / HTML / DOCX
  • Box sets

Look

Story Insights

The book at a glance: words, chapters, reading time, pages, sentence length and how much of it is dialogue. Then the words that carry the book, the rhythm of chapter lengths, and which names are on stage in which chapters.

Everything is counted on your device from the manuscript as it stands. Nothing is sent anywhere, no AI is involved, and nothing is graded.

Keep going

Goals, kept quietly

Set a daily target, a length for the book and a deadline, each on its own, or none at all. The habit calendar shows the words you added day by day, exactly as the log kept them; days before the log began stay blank.

The road so far draws the book's length over time once there are two days to draw, and with a target and a deadline, the steady pace that would get you there. It is labelled as a pace, not a prediction.

  • Daily and book targets
  • Habit calendar
  • The road so far

Nothing is lost

History: what changed since each snapshot

The studio takes a snapshot before every big change, such as an import, the sample story or a restore, and before a chapter is deleted, split or merged. Name a snapshot yourself and it is never removed for you.

Open one to see, section by section, what was added, removed or changed since, with the word count of each change. Looking never changes a word, and you can restore the whole book in one step. Deleting a chapter or a picture comes with Undo.

  • Undo

Share

Beta readers, by the passage

With an account, send a reading link to a frozen copy of a draft. Readers open it in their browser with no account of their own and comment on the paragraph they mean. Their comments come back to your desk, anchored to that passage, and you mark them resolved when you have dealt with them.

You choose whether readers can comment and when the link expires. Republish to show readers your latest draft, or revoke the link at any time.

Accounts & price

Write without an account. Add one when you want your typesetting portal.

Everything above works on your device with no account at all, except the parts marked “with an account”. An account adds:

  • Your shelf: books synced between your devices.
  • An export archive of the files you have made.
  • Beta-reader links and their comments.
  • The official EPUBCheck, run on Beuk's server.
  • PDF/X-1a print conversion on Beuk's server, and the Store-ready? checks that read that file.

Price: a 7-day free trial, then one price — announced before launch. The trial starts when you create your account. Whatever happens to an account, export stays open: your book is always yours to take with you.
