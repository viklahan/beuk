Books set in Beuk, printed by Beuk.

Each book here is a real Beuk book file, typeset in the studio and printed to PDF by Beuk's own print renderer. These are its pages exactly as they came out. Open one and turn the spreads.

What you are looking at

Every page is a page of Beuk's print PDF, rendered at screen size. Nothing was retouched or laid out by hand. The first page of a book stands alone on the right, the way a printed book opens.

How the pictures sit

A picture always takes the left page, edge to edge, and faces its chapter. When a painting is made for two pages, its right half carries on across the gutter and fades away before the first line of text.
