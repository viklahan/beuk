Beuk and Scrivener

Scrivener is a desktop and iOS app for drafting long writing: a binder of sections you can see as cards, an outline or one continuous text, with research kept beside the manuscript and a Compile step that makes the finished files. Beuk is a writing and typesetting studio in the browser that ends in finished EPUB and print files. They overlap in the writing, and differ most in how the book is made at the end.

Every fact about Scrivener on this page comes from Literature & Latte's own website and is linked in the sources below, checked 27 October 2026. Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.

In short

Scrivener may suit you if…

you draft in many small pieces, want corkboard, outline and research beside the text, work on a Mac, a Windows PC or an iPad, and are happy to learn Compile for the finished files.

Beuk may suit you if…

you want the place you write in to typeset the book, with themes instead of compile settings, and to check the EPUB and print files against the stores' own rules before you upload.

Where Scrivener is stronger

  • Structure. The same sections as binder rows, corkboard cards and outliner rows, and Scrivenings to edit several as one text. [1] Beuk has chapters and scenes but no corkboard or outliner.
  • Research beside the draft. Word files, PDFs, images, web pages, sound and video kept in the project. [1]
  • Metadata and saved searches. Labels, status, keywords, custom fields, and collections that list the scenes that still need work. [1]
  • Deadline maths. On the Mac, a draft target and a deadline set the day's session target, from the writing days you choose. [6]
  • A native iOS app, and a longer trial: 30 days of use. [1] [3]

Where Beuk is different

  • Typesetting by theme. Fifteen full-book themes set fonts, rhythm and ornaments together; there are no compile settings to build.
  • Print interiors, spread by spread, with facing pages, plates and page-foot notes, and KDP-style bleed when the book has pictures to the edge.
  • A Store-ready? report against KDP's and IngramSpark's own published print rules, and EPUB presets for eleven stores.
  • A cover maker and print wrap, with the spine worked out from your page count and paper.
  • One studio in the browser on any computer: no download, and no account to start.

What Beuk doesn't do

  • It has no corkboard, no outliner and no Scrivenings view.
  • It keeps no research files beside the manuscript.
  • It has no labels, status, keywords or saved searches.
  • It has no native iOS app, and it does not write screenplays.
  • Its price is not announced yet.
