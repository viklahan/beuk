Beuk and Reedsy Studio

Reedsy Studio and Beuk both run in a web browser and both take a book from writing to EPUB and print-ready PDF. Reedsy Studio's core is free; it adds paid add-ons and real-time collaboration.

Every fact about Reedsy Studio on this page comes from Reedsy Studio's own website and is linked in the sources below, checked 26 October 2026. Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.

In short

Reedsy Studio may suit you if…

you want a free browser-based tool, or you write with a co-author or editor who needs to comment and track changes in real time.

Beuk may suit you if…

you want store presets, store checks, a cover maker and print wrap, and a studio that works offline without an account.

Where Reedsy Studio is stronger

  • A free tier. Reedsy Studio's core writing and formatting features are free. [1] Writing and exporting on your device need no Beuk account; Beuk's account features have a 7-day free trial, then one price.
  • Collaboration. Real-time collaboration, track changes and comments. [1] Beuk has none of these.
  • Planning. Boards for notes, characters, research and outlines. [1]
  • Direct PDF export. [1] Beuk's print PDF is made with your browser's Print → Save as PDF.

Where Beuk is different

  • No account to start. Writing on your device never needs one, and the studio keeps working offline.
  • Eleven EPUB store presets, a Store-ready? report against KDP's and IngramSpark's own print rules, and the package inspector.
  • A cover maker and print wrap, with the spine worked out from your page count and paper.
  • Story Insights: words, rhythm and names on stage, counted on your device.
  • More export formats: Markdown, HTML, DOCX, box sets and ACX narrator scripts.

What Beuk doesn't do

  • Its account features (the shelf, the export archive, beta-reader links and the server checks) have a 7-day free trial, then one price that is not announced yet.
  • It has no real-time co-authoring and no track changes.
  • Without an account it does not make a PDF itself: the print PDF comes from your browser's Print → Save as PDF. With an account, Beuk's server converts the print interior to PDF/X-1a.
