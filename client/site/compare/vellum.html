Beuk and Vellum

Vellum is a Mac app for formatting ebooks and print books. Beuk is a writing and typesetting studio that runs in a browser. Both turn a manuscript into finished files; they go about it differently.

Every fact about Vellum on this page comes from Vellum's own website and is linked in the sources below, checked 26 October 2026. Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.

In short

Vellum may suit you if…

you work on a Mac and want a dedicated desktop app for formatting a finished manuscript, with PDF/X-1a files for print.

Beuk may suit you if…

you want to write and typeset in one place, on any computer, with a cover maker, print wrap and store checks in the same studio.

Where Vellum is stronger

  • A native Mac app. If you work on a Mac and want a desktop program rather than a browser tab, Vellum is built for exactly that. [1]
  • A known, published price. Vellum lists its prices today. [1] Beuk's price is not announced yet.

Where Beuk is different

  • Not tied to one kind of computer. Beuk runs in a web browser, with nothing to install.
  • Writing, not only formatting. Beuk has a writing desk with goals, Story Insights and History, so you can draft and typeset in the same place.
  • A cover maker and print wrap, with the spine worked out from your page count and paper.
  • A Store-ready? report against KDP's and IngramSpark's own published print rules.
  • Beta-reader links, with an account, where readers comment by the paragraph.
  • Checks on Beuk's server, with an account: the print interior converted to PDF/X-1a, and your EPUB run through the official EPUBCheck.

What Beuk doesn't do

  • It has no desktop app: it runs in a browser.
  • Without an account it does not make PDF/X files: the print PDF comes from your browser's Print → Save as PDF. With an account, Beuk's server converts the print interior to PDF/X-1a.
  • Its price is not announced yet. Until it is, we can't tell you how it compares on cost.
