Beuk and Dabble

Dabble is a writing app built around plotting, drafting and editing a novel. Beuk is a writing and typesetting studio that ends in finished EPUB and print files. They overlap in the writing, and differ most at the end.

Every fact about Dabble on this page comes from Dabble's own website and is linked in the sources below, checked 26 October 2026. Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.

In short

Dabble may suit you if…

you are drafting a novel and want plotting tools, co-authoring and editing in one app, and plan to format the finished book elsewhere.

Beuk may suit you if…

you want the same place you write in to make the finished EPUB, the print interior and the cover wrap.

Where Dabble is stronger

  • Plotting. The Plot Grid, a story notebook and character profiles. [1] Beuk has index cards and a list in its Outline, but no plot grid.
  • Working with others. Co-authoring on every plan, and comments and track changes on the Author plan. [1]
  • A longer trial. Every Dabble plan starts with 14 days free. [1] Beuk's trial is 7 days.
  • A lifetime option, from $699 once. [1]

Where Beuk is different

  • Finished books. Beuk exports EPUB with eleven store presets and typeset print interiors; Dabble says it does not export EPUB or PDF directly. [2]
  • Typesetting. Fifteen full-book themes, your own fonts, and a live preview on e-readers and phones.
  • A cover maker and print wrap, with the spine worked out from your page count and paper.
  • A Store-ready? report against KDP's and IngramSpark's own published print rules.
  • No account to start. Writing on your device never needs one.

What Beuk doesn't do

  • It has no plot grid and no story notebook.
  • It has no real-time co-authoring and no track changes.
  • Its price is not announced yet.
