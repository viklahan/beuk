Beuk and Novelcrafter

Novelcrafter is a browser app for planning and drafting a novel around a Codex, a story bible that finds your characters and places in the text, with optional AI through your own key. Beuk is a writing and typesetting studio that ends in finished EPUB and print files. They overlap in the writing, and differ most at the end.

Every fact about Novelcrafter on this page comes from Novelcrafter's own website and is linked in the sources below, checked 27 October 2026. Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.

In short

Novelcrafter may suit you if…

you plan in depth with a story bible and a scenes-by-characters matrix, want AI help with your own key, and will format the finished book in another program.

Beuk may suit you if…

you want the place you write in to make the finished EPUB, the print interior and the cover wrap, and to keep writing when you are offline.

Where Novelcrafter is stronger

  • Planning views. The Matrix lays scenes against characters, locations and subplots like a spreadsheet. [7]
  • AI help, if you want it, with your own key, so you choose the provider and what it costs. [8]
  • Inviting others. Viewers and editors can be invited to a novel on the Specialist plan. [9]
  • A longer trial: 21 days, with every feature unlocked. [1] Beuk's trial is 7 days.

Where Beuk is different

  • Finished books. Typeset EPUB with eleven store presets and print interiors; Novelcrafter's own FAQ says it does not format a novel for publication. [4]
  • Typesetting. Fifteen full-book themes, your own fonts, and a live preview on e-readers and phones.
  • A cover maker and print wrap, with the spine worked out from your page count and paper.
  • A Store-ready? report against KDP's and IngramSpark's own published print rules.
  • Writing offline. The studio keeps working without a connection, and your book is saved on your device as you write.

What Beuk doesn't do

  • It has no planning grid or matrix.
  • It has no AI writing or chat.
  • It has no shared editing of one book by several people.
  • Its price is not announced yet.
