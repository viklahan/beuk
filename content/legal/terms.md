# The terms for using Beuk

These are the ground rules for using Beuk at beuk.studio. They're meant to be read, so they're short. By using the studio, or by creating an account, you agree to them.

## What Beuk is

Beuk is a browser studio that formats manuscripts into books (EPUB, print interiors, Word, Markdown, HTML). It also has optional extras: a shelf that keeps your books on Beuk's server, an export archive, checks run on the server (EPUBCheck, PDF/X), and read-only reading copies you can share with beta readers.

## Free, and no account needed

The studio is free to use, and you can use it fully without an account. An account is optional: create one if you want your books on your shelf, across devices. Keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for what happens under your account.

## Your writing is yours

You keep every right in your manuscripts, covers, pictures and exports. Beuk claims no ownership of them. By putting them on the shelf, in the archive or in a reading copy, you let Beuk store them, process them and show them back to you and to the people you share them with, and keep them in backups. That's only what's needed to run the service, and nothing more. Beuk doesn't use your writing for anything else, and doesn't give it to anyone.

You're responsible for having the right to use what you upload: your own text, and pictures and fonts you're allowed to use.

## Please don't

- Use Beuk for anything illegal, or to store or share content that is illegal where you or your readers are.
- Use reading-copy links to spread spam, malware, harassment or content you have no right to share.
- Try to break into, overload, probe or disrupt the service or other people's accounts, or get around its limits.
- Pretend to be someone else, or sign up for others without their say-so.

If an account breaks these rules, the operator may close it or remove what breaks them.

## Reading copies are yours to look after

When you share a reading copy, anyone who has the link can read it and see its comments. Choose carefully who gets the link: closing it or letting it expire is up to you. What readers write in comments is their own words. Beuk stores them for you but doesn't check them.

## Keep your own copies

Beuk saves your work carefully: on your device first, and on your shelf when you're signed in. The server is backed up. But backups are best effort, not a guarantee. Export a backup now and then, and keep your finished exports somewhere of your own. The studio offers this for exactly that reason.

## As it is

Beuk is offered as it is, and as available. It's looked after with care, but there's no promise that it will always be up, free of mistakes, or that a store or printer will accept a file it makes. Checks like EPUBCheck report what the tool found, and they don't guarantee acceptance. Check your files before you publish them.

## Leaving

You can stop using Beuk at any time. Delete your books, exports and reading copies from the studio whenever you like. To delete your account, write to {{contact}} from the address on it. There's no self-serve button for that yet. The privacy page says how long copies can remain in backups.

These terms may change as Beuk changes. The date at the top shows when they last did. If you keep using Beuk after a change, the new version applies.
