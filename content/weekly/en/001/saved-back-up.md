About a second after you stop typing, the studio saves the whole book in your browser, on your device: the text, its settings, the cover, the pictures and the history.

The indicator in the header says how safe that copy is. Saved, with a tick, means the browser has promised to keep it. Saved · back up means it is saved, but the browser has not made that promise, and could clear it if the device runs short of space. Not saved means it is not.

## What to do about it

Choose the indicator and pick Export a backup. You get the whole book in one file you keep wherever you like, and you can open it again with Open a backup.

> Signed in, with the book on your shelf, every save also goes to the shelf.

When you come back, the studio compares the copy on your device with the copy on your shelf before it shows you the desk. If only one has changed, that one opens; if both have, the studio asks you which to keep, or keeps both.
