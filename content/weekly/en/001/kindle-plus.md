---
title: The Plus Where Unlimited Used to Be
section: The Platform Desk
byline: The Beuk Desk
deck: Amazon folds audiobooks into its subscription, renames the whole thing Kindle+, and leaves the price of a listened page unspoken
---
On October 22, Amazon announced that Kindle Unlimited is now Kindle+, and that KDP audiobooks ride along: United States subscribers can read an ebook or listen to its audiobook at no extra cost, [Written Word Media reported](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/kindle-plus-for-authors/) the same day. For readers little else moves — still $11.99 a month, still twenty titles out at a time, [per Indie Author Magazine's weekly roundup](https://indieauthormagazine.com/acxs-old-payment-model-ends-this-year-this-weeks-publishing-news/). Product pages were re-badged within hours; the old tagline, “Unlimited reading. Over 5 million titles,” now reads “Over 5 million eBooks and audiobooks.”

For authors, the mechanics matter more than the name. Listening is paid by page from the same KDP Select Global Fund that pays for reading, and Amazon says it is adding money to the fund for audiobooks — without saying how much. Each page pays once per subscriber: a chapter listened to and later read does not pay twice. Immersive Reading, where a subscriber reads while listening, pays at the higher applicable rate. No per-format rates have been announced, and Amazon has not committed to keeping ebook per-page rates where they are.

Who is in: every KDP Select ebook stays enrolled with no action. Virtual Voice audiobooks are included automatically once their ebook is in Select — opted in or not. ACX titles qualify only if the ebook is in Select, the audiobook is Audible-exclusive, Whispersync is enabled, and the title uses the Pay for Production model; Royalty Share titles are excluded at launch, with Amazon saying it is “exploring” them.

Dashboards began moving the day before the announcement: an October 21 reporting change gave the KENP report a listened-pages column and a format filter, and relocated Virtual Voice listening on Audible Plus into the Audible royalties column — retroactively — [per Inkfluence AI's running coverage](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/kdp-kenp-page-reads-drop-kindle-plus-2026). Expect apparent cliffs that are mostly money changing rooms, not leaving the house.

:: You need do nothing to stay in. What you must watch: an undisclosed listening rate, an undisclosed fund top-up, and your own marketing — Amazon has published a Kindle+ badge to replace the old one, and “Kindle+ (formerly Kindle Unlimited)” is the honest phrasing while readers catch up. Make no catalog decisions off a dashboard mid-renovation.
