Beuk prints at twenty-four trim sizes, in the four groups the printers themselves use: pocket and mass-market, trade paperback, large and illustrated, and square and landscape.

The smallest is a 4.25 × 7 inch mass-market paperback. The largest are US Letter and A4 for manuals and workbooks, and an 11 × 8.5 inch landscape book for photographs. In between sit the trade sizes most novels use, 6 × 9 inches among them, and A5, the European standard.

## Who prints what

Sixteen of the sizes are ones Amazon KDP prints, between 24 and 828 pages. The other eight are IngramSpark sizes, and the studio says so beside each one, so nobody designs a book for a size their printer will not take.

## The gutter grows with the book

A thick book needs a wider inside margin, or the words disappear into the binding. Beuk's automatic margins start at 0.625 inches for a book of up to 150 pages and step up to 1.125 inches past 700. Writers who know what they want can set their own.

> One list of sizes drives the print interior and the cover wrap, so a trim can never mean one size inside and another on the cover.
