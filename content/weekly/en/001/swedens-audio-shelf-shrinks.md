Bonnier Books, owner of the BookBeat subscription service, has agreed to buy Nextory, [Publishing Perspectives reported](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/09/audiobook-competition-intensifies-in-sweden-as-bonnier-books-owner-of-bookbeat-acquires-nextory/) on September 1. The all-cash price is undisclosed and the deal needs Swedish Competition Authority approval. Nextory carries more than 1.4 million titles. Audiobooks were roughly 29.7% of Sweden’s SEK 5.5 billion book market in 2025, and Storytel is estimated to hold at least half of it.

Bonnier’s Håkan Rudels cited a time “characterized by AI-generated content and international players,” as Spotify and a planned Audible store arrive.

:: If your audio goes wide through an aggregator, fewer, bigger subscription buyers in Europe mean less leverage on terms.
