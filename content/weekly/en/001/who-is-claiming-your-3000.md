---
title: Check Who Is Claiming Your $3,000
section: The Watchdog
byline: The Beuk Desk
deck: Anthropic settlement notices went out this week, and authors found publishers and agents claiming shares of books that belong to them
---
The authors of nearly 500,000 titles are due $3,000 a work from Anthropic’s $1.5 billion copyright settlement, which received final approval in July. [TechCrunch explains the split](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/06/authors-push-back-as-publishers-and-agents-seek-share-of-anthropic-settlement/): a book still in print with a traditional publisher is divided 50-50, while a self-published book, or one whose rights have reverted, should pay the author in full.

This week’s allocation notices showed otherwise for many. On September 4, Writer Beware’s Victoria Strauss [reported two patterns](https://writerbeware.blog/2026/09/04/anthropic-copyright-settlement-publishers-are-making-incorrect-claims-on-authors-payouts/): publishers claiming 50% or 100% of rights-reverted books, and publishers claiming 100% of in-print books where their share should be half. One author found 16 reverted titles claimed. Several literary agencies had filed for 15% to 25%, which Strauss rejects: “Agents are not rightsholders in the books they sell.” She leans toward poor recordkeeping over malice, and Authors Guild CEO Mary Rasenberger told The New York Times, via TechCrunch, that she does not see “a grab by the publishers.” Strauss still calls what she has seen “a peek through a small crack in a massive wall.”

Timing is the catch. [Publishers Lunch reported](https://lunch.publishersmarketplace.com/2026/09/widespread-problems-as-authors-receive-anthropic-settlement-notices/) on September 4 that a first payment of roughly $2,203.56 per title, after fees, is expected between November 1 and 15, but only for “works where all rightsholders are in agreement.” Writer Beware’s September 5 update adds the Authors Guild position that rights must have reverted before August 10, 2022 for a full author claim. By September 6, authors were getting emails offering to buy their claims for “up to” $1,500 a book.

:: A wrong claim you don’t contest keeps your money out of the first payment. Log in even if your notice reports no disagreement; Strauss checked hers and found claims she disputes. Keep your reversion letters and dates to hand, and ignore cash-now offers for your claim.
