title: EPUB 3.4 Waits on the Reading Apps
section: Standards
place: short
order: 3
---
At the W3C working group’s [September 3 call](https://w3c.github.io/pm-wg/minutes/2026-09-03.html), Ivan Herman said the EPUB 3.4 test suite is complete and the group is waiting for results: each feature must be shown working in at least two reading systems. ISO’s SC 34 has asked its members to review the 3.4 Candidate Recommendations “by 2026-09-30.” Ingest tools were invited to run the tests, and an accessibility task force for comics and manga starts October 1.

New readers are still arriving. [FBReader announced](https://fbreader.org/news/202609010000) on September 1 “the first public version of DinoReader,” an app for Android e-ink devices.

:: A 3.4 feature in your file helps only where apps support it. Test new markup in the readers your buyers use before relying on it.
