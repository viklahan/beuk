Etsy's Share & Save program pays enrolled sellers 6.5 percent back on eligible orders from October 20 through January 4, up from the usual 4 percent, [per Inkfluence AI's news roundup](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/self-publishing-news-november-2026). Worth more attention from the same roundup: since September, eligible digital listings in US and Canadian shops can be offered to buyers as printed items through a third-party printer, Ventur Editions — a test that is on by default, with a shop-level opt-out, and no stated answer on what the original seller earns from a print.

:: If you sell printables, planners or workbooks: decide the print toggle deliberately rather than by default, and note the refund window covers the whole gift season.
