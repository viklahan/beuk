The Ramp, at beuk.studio/ramp, is a shelf of nine books. Each is a real Beuk book file, typeset in the studio and printed to PDF by Beuk's own print renderer. The pages are shown exactly as they came out; nothing was retouched or laid out by hand.

## Nine kinds of book

There is a noir thriller, Rain on Calder Street, at 8 × 8 inches. There is a cookbook, Four Kitchens, with a photograph facing every recipe. There is a fantasy novel with a full-page plate before each chapter, a memoir with letters and a diary set apart from the story, and an ergodic novel in which two readers argue in the margins and a page turns sideways.

There are two picture books in landscape, a photo travel book, and a love story at A5 with two letters set apart as notes.

> Every page is a page of Beuk's print PDF, rendered at screen size.

Open one and turn the spreads. The first page stands alone on the right, the way a printed book opens.
