title: Norway's Price Fence
section: The Law & the Ledger

Norway's fixed book price law holds every format at a set price for 12 months, and during that window ebooks and digital audiobooks can only be bought individually — keeping new releases out of subscription services entirely, [per Publishing Perspectives](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/11/around-the-book-world-monday-november-9-2026/). BookBeat's Niclas Sandin and Nextory's Jacob Martinelle say it strangles streaming growth; supporters credit it with sustaining 460 Norwegian bookshops against Sweden's 290. The loophole is instructive: English-language audiobooks are exempt, and make up 18 percent of Norwegian audiobook consumption versus 6 percent in Sweden. Poland is discussing a similar law.

:: If you sell into Europe, subscription is not the whole market everywhere — and where it's fenced out, à la carte and direct sales carry the new release. Price your launches accordingly, market by market.
