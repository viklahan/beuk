United is the first airline to let passengers log into their own Spotify accounts on seatback screens and resume audiobooks mid-chapter, announced November 5, [per Publishing Perspectives](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/11/around-the-book-world-monday-november-9-2026/). In the same roundup, an unlikely data point from Seoul: South Korea's “text hip” trend — reading as fashion statement — shows up in Samsung Card figures reported by *The Korea Times* on November 4: bookstore sales up 8.5 percent in the first nine months of 2026, and two million literary-themed cream buns sold by publisher Minumsa and convenience chain GS25 in under two months.

:: The surfaces where books get consumed keep multiplying — seatbacks, convenience stores, social feeds. Wide audio distribution catches the first kind; a cover pretty enough to photograph catches the second.
