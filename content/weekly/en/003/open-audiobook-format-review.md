section: Standards

---
The [minutes of the W3C Publishing Maintenance Working Group](https://w3c.github.io/pm-wg/minutes/2026-09-17.html) for September 17 are unusually candid. Hadrien Gardeur said the W3C audiobooks specification “has failed as both” an interchange format and a format for listeners. Brady Duga reported that publishers had told Play Books they would use the Kobo format instead. Ivan Herman said “EPUB annotations will not make this cycle.” Wendy Reid will step down as chair at the end of the charter, and Duga joins as chair.

:: There is no shared open audiobook package the stores rely on. Deliver audio to each retailer in the format that retailer asks for, and keep your master files so you can repackage later.
