Answering Thomas Umstattd Jr. with Kris Austin, “[Should You Leave Kindle Unlimited in 2026?](https://www.authormedia.com/should-you-leave-kindle-unlimited-in-2026/),” Novel Marketing, Author Media, 16 September 2026.

This week’s Novel Marketing is a fair-minded hour on an old argument. Thomas Umstattd Jr. is plain that “many authors make less money going wide, especially in the short term,” and that genre decides a lot: some romance and LitRPG readers live inside Kindle Unlimited. “There’s no one right answer for everyone,” he says, “but there is a right answer for you right now.” His guest, Kris Austin, is CEO and co-founder of Draft2Digital, a wide distributor, and listeners should weigh his advice with that in mind. Even so, he calls wide “a long-term play” that is “generally not instant.”

We agree with the episode’s honesty, and we would push harder on one line. Umstattd says he should “never let another company decide whether my company still exists.” Inside KDP Select, one company runs the account, the algorithm and the payout. Umstattd puts the fund at $700 million a year, and his own image is a machine that “tells you how much money you get and what to write next.” None of that is yours to negotiate.

The episode also brings news worth checking: both men say KU authors can now be in libraries too. If that holds for your books, it is a cheap first step outside one store.

Staying exclusive can be the right call. Staying exclusive because you never looked again is not. Put the question in your calendar and answer it again each time it comes round.
