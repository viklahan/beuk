Victoria Strauss at [Writer Beware reported on September 18](https://writerbeware.blog/2026/09/18/love-v-wiley-book-writers-a-publishing-scam-gets-a-rare-comeuppance/) that author Raymond Love paid Wiley Book Writers, which advertised on Facebook, nearly $50,000 and received $3,000 and 50 hardcovers in return. The pitch included fake “HarperCollins” distribution deals. On August 26 a court entered a $75,000 judgment against four defendants, and there is a default judgment for the full $190,025 requested against Techture and Muhammad Khan. Whether any of it can be collected is uncertain.

:: A famous publisher’s name attached to a package you pay for is the tell. Check with that publisher directly before you pay anyone.
