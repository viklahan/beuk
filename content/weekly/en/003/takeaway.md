Selling print abroad? Work out your list price in each market after local tax before you set it.

Sharing an Anthropic settlement claim? Settle the split with your co-claimants before the 60 days run out.

Making an audiobook? Follow each retailer’s own delivery spec, and keep your master audio files.

In KDP Select? Decide on purpose whether to stay, and check whether libraries are now open to you.
