Not this week's news but October's, filed here because it will outlast the week: EPUB 3.4, Reading Systems 3.4 and Accessibility 1.2 sit at W3C Candidate Recommendation, and EPUBCheck 5.4.0 already validates against them, [writes Wendy Reid of the Publishing Maintenance Working Group](https://www.w3.org/blog/2026/were-on-a-roll-epub-3-4-is-coming/). New in the checker: “roll” scrolling layouts for webtoon-style books, AVIF and JPEG XL images, OPUS and AAC audio, and an accessibility contact-email field. Font obfuscation, NCX and the old OPF *guide* element are formally outdated — discouraged, though reading systems must still support them. The comfort, from the spec itself: an EPUB that conforms to an earlier version conforms to 3.4 as it stands.

:: Revalidate against 5.4.0, prefer WOFF2 for embedded fonts, and treat “outdated” warnings as a to-do list, not an emergency. Nothing breaks today.
