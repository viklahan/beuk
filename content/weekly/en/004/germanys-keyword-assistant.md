MVB, which runs Germany’s VLB books-in-print database, will build Snackz’s AI “Metadaten-Assistent” into the VLB interface, [Börsenblatt reported](https://www.boersenblatt.net/home/mvb-kooperiert-mit-snackz-441873) on September 23. It generates classifications, keywords and SEO text from a manuscript or synopsis, will launch in the fourth quarter as a paid add-on for publishers and self-publishers, runs on EU servers, and Börsenblatt reports the data is “niemals zum Training von KI-Modellen verwendet” (never used to train AI models).

:: Keywords and categories decide whether a book is found. Before you feed a manuscript to any metadata tool, get the no-training promise in writing, as this one states it.
