Writer Beware’s Victoria Strauss [took apart the personalised scam pitches](https://writerbeware.blog/2026/09/25/how-those-personalized-generative-ai-scam-emails-are-built) filling authors’ inboxes on September 25. Scammers scrape author websites, retailer listings and reviews into AI templates with placeholders. One example posed as a HarperCollins editor writing from a Gmail address; another raw output tagged its target “Warm / Cooperatively Engaged” and set a $150 “production allocation.” Stock openers recur: “I recently had the opportunity” and “What stood out most was.”

Her advice is to check the sender’s domain, then delete and block. As she puts it, “familiarity breeds suspicion.”

:: The more of your book is public, the more convincing these emails get. A real publisher writes from its own domain.
