---
title: What an Indie Audio Catalogue Fetches
section: The Money Desk
byline: The Beuk Desk
deck: Private equity buys Podium, the audiobook house built on genre fiction and independent authors, at a reported price per title well above the last big audio sale
---
Podium Entertainment has changed hands. On September 23, [Latham & Watkins announced](https://www.lw.com/en/news/2026/09/latham-advises-flexpoint-ford-in-acquisition-of-podium-entertainment) that it had advised Flexpoint Ford on the acquisition, made with Shamrock Capital from Presidio Investors, with GoldState Music taking a minority stake. The firm calls Podium “the third-largest audiobook publisher in North America.”

Terms were not disclosed, but [Publishing Perspectives reported](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/09/around-the-book-world-september-28-2026/) on September 28 that The Wall Street Journal valued the deal at more than $400 million. Podium first gained attention producing the audiobook of Andy Weir’s self-published *The Martian*, and now has “a catalogue of more than 15,000 titles, largely in genre fiction and from independent authors.” Columnist Carlo Carrenho did the arithmetic: if the reported price holds, that is roughly $26,700 a title, against about $15,200 when RBmedia sold in 2023, a rise of “more than 70%” on what he calls a “very crude measure.” Catalogues, he adds, “are not created equal.”

The trade numbers point the same way. The [AAP’s July StatShot](https://publishers.org/news/aap-july-2026-statshot-report-overall-publishing-industry-up-14-0-for-the-month-of-july-and-up-5-4-year-to-date/), released September 22, put U.S. digital audio revenue at $103.1 million for the month, up 12.4%, and $700.9 million year to date, up 12.6%. Ebooks fell 3.4% in July to $85.3 million and are down 3.1% for the year. These are reported revenues from more than 1,416 publishers, not indie sales, but the direction is plain.

:: Investors are paying more for each audiobook in a genre catalogue than they did three years ago. If someone offers to buy or license your audio rights, ask what the term and reversion clauses are worth to them before you sign. If you are keeping your audio rights, they are now an asset with a market price.
