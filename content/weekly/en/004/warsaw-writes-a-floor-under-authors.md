title: Warsaw Writes a Floor Under Authors
section: The Wide World

Poland’s Culture Minister has signed a request to add the draft book market protection law to the government’s legislative work list, [Rynek Książki reported](https://rynek-ksiazki.pl/aktualnosci/ustawa-o-ochronie-rynku-ksiazki-trafi-do-wykazu-prac-legislacyjnych-rzadu/) on September 22. Public consultation starts once the Government Legislation Centre approves it. The draft caps distributor discounts at 45% of the uniform price, sets a minimum of 10% of cover price for authors and 3% for translators, covers ebooks and audiobooks as well as print, and creates automated sales reporting.

[Publishing Perspectives](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/09/around-the-book-world-september-28-2026/), citing the ministry, says the fixed price would hold for 12 months after release, and that the author minimum applies where contracts are based on cover price.

:: A year-long fixed price that covers ebooks would limit discount promotions in Poland. If you sell wide into that market, follow the bill before planning launch pricing there.
