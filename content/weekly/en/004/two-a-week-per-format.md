title: Two a Week, Per Format
section: The Watchdog

From Monday, September 21, KDP accounts can create two new titles per format each week, down from ten, [ALLi’s Dan Holloway reported](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/amazon-cuts-kdp-weekly-upload-limit/) on September 24. Ebooks, paperbacks and hardcovers count separately, existing books are unaffected, and Amazon’s notice says “your weekly limit resets every Sunday at 12:00 AM UTC.” Its only stated reason is “to help maintain a positive publishing experience for all authors.”

Citing The Bookseller, [Publishing Perspectives](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/09/around-the-book-world-september-28-2026/) adds that the cap applies to every KDP user “regardless of how their books are created,” and that Amazon “declined to say whether AI-generated content was behind the move.” Holloway notes the cap will not feel positive for anyone with “a large backlist to start getting out there.”

:: Box-set splits, reverted-rights reissues and backlist relaunches now need a weekly upload calendar. Amazon has published no reasoning beyond one sentence.
