Oxford University Press [updated its AI guidelines](https://corp.oup.com/news/supporting-researchers-to-disclose-ai-use-with-confidence/) on September 10 for both books and journal articles. Authors are expected to be transparent about “any significant use of AI tools” and remain responsible for checking anything a tool generated. Its survey of more than 2,600 researchers found 74% unclear on what to disclose, 44% worried that disclosure would hurt how their work is seen, and only 36% keeping a record of their AI use.

:: Publishers, retailers and platforms are each writing their own disclosure rules, and Spotify’s label shows they are arriving in trade books too. Keep a short note per book of which tools touched it and for what. You can’t disclose what you didn’t record.
