Jerziobuk, a Peruvian audiobook startup, launched October 30, selling individual titles with a subscription planned as the catalog grows, [per Publishing Perspectives](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/11/around-the-book-world-monday-november-9-2026/). Founders Pablo Alonso Cotrina and Aldo Incio plan publisher distribution deals and audiobook production services, and eventually original content — a small new storefront in a Spanish-language audio market the giants have barely begun to serve.

:: Every regional storefront is shelf space the big platforms don't own. Keep your audio rights non-exclusive where you can, so your catalog can walk through doors like this one when they open.
