The W3C published a new [Candidate Recommendation Draft of EPUB Accessibility 1.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-epub-a11y-12-20260912/) on September 12, edited by people from DAISY, Benetech and Fondazione LIA. The specification applies to an EPUB of any version, and every conforming file must at least carry the accessibility metadata that lets a store or reader see what the book offers.

:: That metadata is what a buyer using a screen reader sees before purchase. If your export tool writes it, check that it describes your book truthfully.
