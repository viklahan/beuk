On September 9 Spotify for Authors [launched Audiobook Creation Tools](https://authors.spotify.com/blog/creationtools) in beta. It is open to self-published authors in the US writing in English. You upload an EPUB, pick a digital voice built with ElevenLabs, and the tool produces an audiobook you review before release, with pronunciation rules to correct how words are read.

Spotify says you can “create up to five audiobooks for free.” The small print matters: “A validated payment profile is required for access, and some limits on usage and audiobook generation apply.” Distribution is non-exclusive, through Spotify’s Voices by INaudio service, and you can publish or unpublish whenever you like. Every title made this way “will be clearly labeled for listeners on the platform” as digitally narrated.

Spotify’s pitch is the gap between formats: it counts “only about one million audiobooks” against “over forty million ebooks.” Its promise to authors is control: “Nothing goes live until it’s right.”

:: A backlist title with no audio edition can now have one free, within Spotify’s limits, on non-exclusive terms. But you are the proofreader. Listen to every chapter before you release it, because the label tells listeners a machine read it and a mispronounced hero name will be heard by all of them.
