A week after the Kindle+ rename, author dashboards are still arguing with themselves. Since the October 21 reporting change, KENP figures have shown drops of 40 to 50 percent — mostly, [per Inkfluence AI's reconstruction](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/kdp-kenp-page-reads-drop-kindle-plus-2026), Virtual Voice listening moved retroactively into the Audible royalties column, not money lost. But an October 31 KDP FAQ claiming the changes primarily affected Virtual Voice beta participants squares poorly with the field: some authors with no Virtual Voice titles saw drops too, unexplained, while support calls the figures a glitch in one chat and correct in the next. September's fund paid $69.3 million; what a listened page will earn, and how much Amazon adds for audio, remain unsaid.

:: The October fund statement, due mid-November, is the first real test of what this transition pays. Until then: save dated reports, check the Audible column before mourning, and make no Select decisions over reporting alone.
