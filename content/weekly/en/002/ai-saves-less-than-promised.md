A Frankfurt Book Fair report written by Dosdoce.com, *Who is narrating the future? AI adoption, use and outlook in audio production*, interviewed 85 people from audio studios, publishers and platforms across Europe, the Americas, Africa and the Middle East. [PublishNews reported its findings](https://www.publishnews.com.br/materias/2026/09/09/feira-de-frankfurt-publica-relatorio-sobre-uso-de-inteligencia-artificial-em-audiolivros) on September 9: 85% use AI somewhere in production, 17% in more than six stages, and 15% stay strictly human.

Vendors promise savings of 85% to 95%. Respondents reported 20% in their second year and 50% in their third. Voice quality and copyright transparency were top concerns for 86% when choosing a supplier. “The headline number and the operational reality are two different stories,” said Dosdoce founder Javier Celaya (our translation from the Portuguese).

:: When a narration vendor quotes a saving, ask what the producers in this study actually got. And ask any AI voice supplier where its voices came from.
