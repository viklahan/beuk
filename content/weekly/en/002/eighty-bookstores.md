Japanese distributor Tohan's HONYAL program, built to help small bookstores open, has helped launch 80 new shops in two years, with 105 now operating and 44 more contracted, per a Tohan announcement dated October 30 carried by [Publishing Perspectives](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/11/around-the-book-world-monday-november-9-2026/). The program offers weekly deliveries, limited returns and waived security deposits; 78 percent of participating stores have under 33 square meters of selling space.

:: Tiny retail is the one bookstore channel a small press can actually court — hand-sellable stock, consignment-scale relationships. The future of hand-selling looks small, local, and open to introductions.
