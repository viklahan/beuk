A Wall Street Journal report, summarized by Good e-Reader and carried in [Indie Author Magazine's roundup](https://indieauthormagazine.com/acxs-old-payment-model-ends-this-year-this-weeks-publishing-news/), says YouTube has been talking with major publishers about an official audiobook offering that could launch as early as 2027, possibly inside YouTube Premium. Nothing is announced, and it is unclear whether independent authors could take part.
