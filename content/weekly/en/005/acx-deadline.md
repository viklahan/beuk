section: Money & Margins

Buried in the week's news, a deadline with teeth: audiobook authors with Audible titles have until December 31 to enroll them in ACX's new payment model, [Indie Author Magazine reports](https://indieauthormagazine.com/acxs-old-payment-model-ends-this-year-this-weeks-publishing-news/), citing a guide from the Alliance of Independent Authors. The legacy model ends January 1, 2027, and titles still on the old terms will be withdrawn. The new model raises headline royalty rates — but membership listening is now paid from a shared “Member Value” amount, and ALLi's warning deserves framing on the wall: a higher percentage of a different base is not automatically a raise.

:: A withdrawn backlist earns nothing. Run the numbers title by title before the holidays eat your calendar, and treat the headline rate as the start of the math, not the end.
