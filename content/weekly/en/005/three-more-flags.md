Kobo Plus went live in Cyprus, Malta and Slovakia on November 11 — €9.99 a month to read or listen, €12.99 for both, 14-day trials — two weeks after launching in Estonia, Lithuania and Slovenia, [per Inkfluence AI's November news roundup](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/self-publishing-news-november-2026). The same week, per that roundup: readers gained the ability to borrow from CloudLibrary-connected libraries straight to a Kobo e-reader (November 12), and Kobo's 2026 Book Report (November 10) — the subject of last week's deep dive — kept confirming that readers find books subgenre-first rather than through single viral titles.

:: If you distribute wide through Kobo Writing Life or an aggregator, your subscription footprint grew again this month without you lifting a finger — confirm your territories and Kobo Plus enrollment. And subgenre-first discovery rewards precise categories and keywords over broad ones.
