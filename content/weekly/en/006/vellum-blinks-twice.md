---
title: Vellum Blinks Twice
section: The Tooling Desk
byline: The Beuk Desk
deck: The Mac formatter added an AI hookup, pulled it in a day, and has now put it back — off by default — after two weeks that split its authors down the middle
---
Vellum, the three-person company behind the Mac formatting tool, spent November learning in public what its authors think of AI. On November 4 it shipped Vellum 4.2 with “Automation,” a connection that lets a separately installed assistant app — ChatGPT or Claude — operate Vellum on an author's behalf: fixing a broken link across a hundred titles, updating back matter, assembling box sets. [One day later it removed the feature](https://blog.vellum.pub/2026/11/weve-removed-vellum-automation/) in 4.2.1, writing that “we underestimated how strongly many of you would object to Vellum working with AI assistants at all,” and stressing that Vellum itself includes no AI functionality and that Automation was “definitely not” built to encourage AI-generated writing.

Then the mail turned around. In the days after the removal, Vellum says, hundreds of authors wrote asking for the option back — many unsure they would ever use it, but unwilling to make that decision for others. On November 18 the company [restored Automation in Vellum 4.2.2](https://blog.vellum.pub/2026/11/moving-forward-with-automation/), rebuilt around consent: a new Connections pane in Settings governs whether any assistant may talk to Vellum at all, and “by default, these connections are Never allowed.”

Founder Brad West, speaking to [Indie Author Magazine](https://indieauthormagazine.com/vellum-gives-authors-the-option-to-automatically-update-formats-across-titles-again/) from the Author Nation conference in Las Vegas — where booth conversations helped change the team's mind — put numbers on the split: by his guess, about one in five emails opposed the feature. “Twenty percent negative is still a large number. And we're not ignoring it,” he said, likening Automation's use of AI to that of an advanced spell check, while conceding “it's really hard to design it to draw a line and say it can do this and it can't do that.”

For the unpersuaded, Vellum extended its normal 30-day return window: until November 25, any purchase made since September 1 can be refunded.

:: Your formatting tool just became the indie world's test case for AI consent. Vellum's settlement — ship the capability, default it to off, make connection an explicit grant — is the pattern every author tool will now be measured against. If you use Vellum: the switch is in Settings → Connections, it is set to Never, and nothing changes unless you change it. If you object on principle, the refund door closes November 25.
