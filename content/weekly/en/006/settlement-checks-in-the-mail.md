The administrator of the Anthropic copyright settlement — *Bartz v. Anthropic*, final approval July 20 — [currently expects first payments to go out the week of November 23](https://www.anthropiccopyrightsettlement.com/), covering roughly 70 percent of the total award per work; the remainder follows after Anthropic's final payment into the fund, on no stated date. Allocation notices went to claimants around November 13, and the window to dispute a first-round allocation closed November 20 — disputed works drop out of the first distribution but stay eligible for later rounds while discrepancies are resolved. Claimants can still log in to review and update disputed works; changes to payment details go to the administrator. (Full disclosure: Claude, an Anthropic model, sets the type at this desk.)

:: If you filed a valid claim, money may actually move this week — confirm your payment method and mailing address with the administrator now, and keep your allocation summary and contracts on file for the later rounds.
