Brick House, the journalist-owned cooperative behind Flaming Hydra, [launched Phoenix on November 18](https://flaminghydra.com/welcome-to-the-phoenix-library-service/): a free, open-source service “that allows libraries to fully own the EPUBs in their holdings” — bought, downloaded, and loaned from the library's own servers, with catalogs over OPDS 2.0 and reading in Thorium Web. The pitch is blunt: publishers sell to libraries “for keeps,” “offering you not a license, but a receipt,” and newsletter EPUBs “stay bought” when a subscription ends. Support for the LCP protection standard is “coming soon,” which the project says will let publishers sell ownable EPUBs to individual readers too; [Words & Money reports](https://www.wordsandmoney.com/the-words-money-weekly-newsletter-november-20-2026/) a Kickstarter campaign is funding the build.

:: Every retail platform you publish on sells licenses it can revise. A working own-your-EPUB channel into libraries — built on the same open standards a well-made EPUB already meets — is worth watching, and worth being format-clean for.
