Vellum user with a view on Automation? The switch lives in Settings → Connections (default: Never) — and the extended refund window for purchases since September 1 closes November 25.

Filed in the Anthropic settlement? Payments are expected this week at ~70 percent of your award — verify your payment method and address with the administrator, and file away your allocation summary.

Run DAISY's ACE checker on your EPUB and write honest alt text now — WCAG 2.1 AA is the bar the EAA and US Title II both point at, and Penguin Random House says it may take several years to get most of its ebooks there.

Keep dated drafts and revision history for every manuscript you submit anywhere — provenance records are quietly becoming the currency of AI-detection disputes.

Selling direct or wide? Keep your EPUB standards-clean (valid, no exotic hacks) — ownership-based channels like Phoenix are built on open standards: OPDS 2.0 catalogs, the Thorium Web reader.
