Early drafts travel. When a page turns up somewhere it shouldn't, you want to know whose copy it came from — without spying on anyone's reading.

## Steps
1. The add-reader form says it plainly: **each reader's name shows on every page of their copy**. The page reads "Shared with" the reader's name and the date the link was made.
   say: The form says it plainly: each reader's name shows on every page of their copy. The page reads, shared with, then the reader's name, and the date their link was made.
   do: show "Each reader's name shows on every page"
2. Behind the text sits a faint stamp of the reader's name. It stays when the page is printed, so even a copy on paper says who it was shared with. The comment form carries the name too.
   say: Behind the text sits a faint stamp of the reader's name. It stays when the page is printed, so even a copy on paper says who it belongs to. And their comments arrive already signed.
   do: show "Readers"
3. That is the whole record. Each reader's row shows when they were added and when their link was first and last opened — no pages, no time spent, no network address. A reader is never shown your email address.
   say: And that is the whole record. Beuk keeps when a reader was added and when their link was opened. No pages read, no time spent, no network address, and your email is never shown to them.
   do: show "Readers"

## Takeaway
Enough to know whose copy went where; nothing that watches your readers read.
