Before a book ships, someone else should read it. This course is six short lessons on Beuk's beta-reader links: a frozen, read-only copy your readers open in any browser with no account, a link of their own for each named reader, their comments landing back beside your manuscript, and a password-locked copy for the editor who should be the only one to open it. Every lesson uses the sample novella, *The Lantern Coast*, so you can follow along on the same book, or on your own.
