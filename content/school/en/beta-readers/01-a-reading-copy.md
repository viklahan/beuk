title: A reading copy
blurb: Make a read-only link, copy it once, and know exactly what your reader will see.

A reading copy is your book, frozen at this moment, behind a link. Readers open it in any browser, on any phone, with no account and no app. Your manuscript stays on your desk; they get the copy.

## Steps
1. Signed in, with the book on your shelf, open **Export** and find **Share with beta readers**.
   say: Signed in, with the book on your shelf, open Export. The share section is called Share with beta readers.
   do: click "Export"; wait 1; show "Share with beta readers"
2. Choose when the link expires under **Link expires**, whether **Readers can comment**, and whether to **Ask readers for their name**. Then choose **Create a reading link**.
   say: Choose when the link expires, whether readers can comment, and whether to ask each reader for their name. Then create the reading link.
   do: show "Link expires"; show "Readers can comment"; click "Create a reading link"; wait 2
3. **Copy link** now. The server keeps only a fingerprint of the link, never the link itself, so only this device can show it again.
   say: Copy the link now. The server keeps only a fingerprint of it, never the link itself, so only this device can show it again.
   do: click "Copy link"; wait 1
4. The reader sees the copy as it was when you shared it. Your later edits stay yours until you choose **Republish**, which re-renders the copy from the book's current text.
   say: Your reader sees the copy as it was when you shared it. Keep writing all you like; nothing reaches them until you press Republish.
   do: show "Republish"

## Takeaway
The copy is frozen and the manuscript is yours. Republish when, and only when, you want readers on the newest draft.
