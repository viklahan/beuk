---
title: A link for each reader
blurb: Name your readers and each one gets a link of their own, with its own expiry and its own close.
guide: share
---
One link for everyone works until you want to know who is reading, or need to cut one person off without cutting off the rest. Named links give each reader a link that is theirs alone.

## Steps
1. Every reading copy has a **Readers** list. It starts empty; the copy's open link serves anyone who has it.
   say: Every reading copy has a list of readers. It starts empty, and the copy's open link serves anyone who holds it.
   do: show "Readers"
2. Choose **Add a reader** and give their name, with an expiry of their own. A copy takes up to fifty named readers.
   say: Add a reader by name, and give their link an expiry of its own. A copy takes up to fifty named readers.
   do: click "Add a reader"; wait 1; show "Reader's name"
3. Each reader's row has its own copy and close, by name, and says when they were added and when their link was first and last opened. Once a named link exists, **Close the open link** shuts the shared one; the named links stay open.
   say: Each reader's row has its own copy and close, by name, and says when their link was first and last opened. Once a named link exists, you can close the open link, and the named links stay open.
   do: show "Readers"

## Takeaway
One reader per link means one expiry per link and one close per link. The group link can go quiet while every named reader keeps reading.
