Every reading copy ends, by your hand or by its expiry date. Closing one is quiet, deliberate, and keeps everything your readers already gave you.

## Steps
1. **Revoke** closes a reading copy. It asks before it acts, and if the copy still has named links, it says so — revoking closes those too. A closed link can't be opened again.
   say: Revoke closes a reading copy. It asks before it acts, and if the copy still has named readers, it tells you their links close with it. A closed link can't be opened again.
   do: show "Revoke"
2. A link can also close itself: the expiry you chose under **Link expires** when you made it, or the one each named reader carries. The list shows each copy as Open, Revoked or Expired.
   say: A link can also close itself, on the expiry you chose when you made it. The list shows each copy as open, revoked, or expired.
   do: show "Link expires"
3. After the end, a reader meets a calm page — "This reading copy is closed" — not an error. A mistyped link says "We couldn't find this reading copy". Comments already left stay with you.
   say: After the end, a reader meets a calm page. This reading copy is closed. Never an error screen. And every comment they left stays with you.
   do: show "Revoke"

## Takeaway
Closing is tidy and final: the reader gets a courteous page, you keep the comments, and a fresh link takes a minute to make.
