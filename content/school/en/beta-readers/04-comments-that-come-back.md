A reader selects a passage and chooses Comment on this, or comments on the chapter as a whole — no account needed. Everything they write comes home to your studio.

## Steps
1. Open **Reader comments** in the sidebar. The window says what it holds: **what your beta readers said**, anchored to the passage they were reading, grouped by section in book order.
   say: Open Reader comments, in the sidebar. What your beta readers said, anchored to the passage they were reading, grouped by section in the order of the book.
   do: click "Reader comments"; wait 1; show "What your beta readers said"
2. **Jump to passage** opens the editor on that chapter with the quoted words selected, so the comment and the sentence it is about sit in front of you together.
   say: Jump to passage opens the editor on that chapter, with the quoted words selected, so the comment and the sentence it is about sit in front of you together.
   do: show "Jump to passage"
3. Mark a comment **Resolve** once you've dealt with it, or **Reopen** it; **Show resolved** brings the finished ones back. On a named link, each reader sees only their own comments, never another reader's name or words.
   say: Resolve a comment once you've dealt with it, and show the resolved ones again any time. On a named link, each reader sees only their own comments, never another reader's.
   do: show "Show resolved"

## Takeaway
Feedback arrives where you can act on it: beside the manuscript, pinned to the words it quotes, resolved one by one as the draft improves.
