Uploading a book should be an anticlimax. This course is six lessons on the machinery between your manuscript and a store's catalogue: the eleven presets and what each one actually changes inside the package, the validator and the Package Inspector that read your file with a store's scepticism, the official EPUBCheck on Beuk's server, how to read a rejection written by a machine, and the rights notice on the copyright page. Every lesson uses the sample novella, *The Lantern Coast*, so you can follow along on the same book, or on your own.
