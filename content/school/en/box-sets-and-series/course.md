A trilogy in one volume sells the whole story at once, and a series only works if Book Five remembers Book Two. This course is five lessons on books that travel in company: the Covenant, the series bible that lives with the book and can be shared across a series, and the Box Set builder that binds finished books into one EPUB — each volume with its own cover, its own pictures and its own title, under a single table of contents. Every lesson uses the sample novella, *The Lantern Coast*, so you can follow along on the same book, or on your own.
