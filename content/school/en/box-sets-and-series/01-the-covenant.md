By Book Three, somebody's eyes have quietly changed colour. The Covenant is where a book — and a series — keeps its facts: who everyone is, what changed and where, written once and kept with the book instead of in a drawer.

## Steps
1. Open **Covenant** in the sidebar. Its entries are the characters, places, things, groups and lore of your book — a name, a line, notes, a picture, quick facts like Age or Status, and links between entries ("sister of"). Start from the names Story Insights found, or by hand. Every name the Covenant knows is faintly underlined on the desk; point at one or press Alt+Enter for its card, and each card shows every passage where the name appears.

2. When something changes in the story — Mara is hurt, the keep falls — select the words and press Alt+M to **mark a change**, pinned to the passage where it happens. An entry's **Right now** shows the latest change at or before your cursor, and **Story so far** lists them all in story order; changes still ahead of you show greyed, as not yet true here.

3. For a series, open **Project Settings** and give each book the same **Series** name and a **Number in series**. When your signed-in books share a series name, Beuk offers one card — share one Covenant? — and nothing joins until you say yes, entry by entry. From then on, Right now and Story so far read across the books in series order, so Book Seven knows what happened to Mara in Book Five.

## Takeaway
The Covenant is for you, not for the reader: nothing in it reaches an EPUB, a print file, a Word export or your word count. It is the one notebook that travels with the series.
