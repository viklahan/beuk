The running head is the line at the top of each page, usually the title or the author. Page numbers, the folios, sit with it. Readers barely notice them when they are right, and always notice when they are wrong.

## Steps
1. In **Export**, under **Print**, choose what the **Running head** shows: the book title and author, the book title, the author name, the chapter title, or nothing. **Head placement** puts it at the top or the bottom of the page.
   say: In Export, under Print, choose what runs across the page: the title and the author, just one of them, the chapter title, or nothing at all. And choose whether it sits at the top of the page or the bottom.
   do: click "Export"; wait 1; click "Print"; wait 1; show "Running head"; show "Head placement"
2. **No head on opening pages** keeps the first page of every chapter and section clear, as books do. **No head or folio on blank pages** leaves the blank page before a chapter completely bare.
   say: Opening pages stay clear of the running head, as they do in printed books. And the blank page before a chapter can stay completely bare, with no head and no page number.
   do: show "No head on opening pages"
3. Put the **Page numbers** at the bottom centre or the bottom outer corner, or leave them off.
   say: Last, the page numbers: bottom centre, the bottom outer corner, or none.
   do: show "Page numbers"

## Takeaway
A back-matter page, like About the author, carries its own title in the head, not the last chapter's. You don't have to set that; Beuk does it.
