Your ebook and your paperback come from the same manuscript. This course is the print half: six short lessons on the settings in Export that turn your book into pages, and the checks that catch a problem before the printer does. Every lesson uses the sample novella, *The Lantern Coast*, so you can follow along on the same book, or on your own.
