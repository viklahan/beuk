Readers meet the cover before they meet a sentence, and stores show it smaller than a postage stamp. In Beuk the cover is a real part of the book: declared in the EPUB, shown first, and recognised by every store's catalogue.

## Steps
1. Open **Project Settings**. The **Cover Image** block takes a JPG or a PNG, and shows the cover the book has now.
   say: Open Project Settings. The Cover Image block takes a J P G or a P N G, and shows the cover your book has now.
   do: click "Project Settings"; wait 1; show "Cover Image"
2. **Upload Cover** takes artwork of your own. For store catalogues, bigger is better: Kindle recommends 2560 × 1600 px. A small image uploads fine, but a store may refuse it later.
   say: Upload Cover takes artwork of your own. Bigger is better: Kindle recommends twenty five sixty by sixteen hundred pixels. A small image uploads fine, but a store may refuse it later.
   do: show "Upload Cover"
3. No artwork yet? **Make a Cover** opens the Cover Maker, which starts from your book: the title, subtitle, author and series are already filled in, and a cover made here follows the book when its title changes.
   say: No artwork yet? Make a Cover opens the Cover Maker, and it starts from your book: the title, subtitle, author and series are already filled in.
   do: show "Make a Cover"
4. However the cover arrives, it is carried properly: the EPUB gets the image, a cover page first in the reading order, and a cover landmark in the navigation — the thing screen readers and store ingesters look for. Remove it, and the package returns to exactly its coverless self.
   say: However the cover arrives, the EPUB gets the image, a cover page first in the reading order, and a cover landmark in the navigation. Remove it, and the package returns to exactly its coverless self.
   do: show "Cover Image"

## Takeaway
A book without a cover still exports complete and valid — the checks note it as a friendly warning, never an error. But the cover is the first page every store shows, so give it one before the book travels.
