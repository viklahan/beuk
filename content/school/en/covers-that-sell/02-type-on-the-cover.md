At full size a cover is art; at store size it is type. The Cover Maker treats each line — title, subtitle, author, tagline, series — as a block of its own, with its own face, colour and place.

## Steps
1. Open the Cover Maker from **Project Settings** → **Make a Cover**. Under **Text Element**, pick the block you are setting: Title, Subtitle, Author, Tagline or Series Line.
   say: Open the Cover Maker from Project Settings. Under Text Element, pick the block you are setting: title, subtitle, author, tagline or series line.
   do: click "Project Settings"; wait 1; click "Make a Cover"; wait 2; show "Text Element"
2. Each block has its own **Font**, **Colour** and **Size**. Three display faces ship with Beuk, all open-licensed and self-hosted: **Pinyon Script** (a formal script), **Patrick Hand** (handwriting, for children's books) and **Abril Fatface** (a heavy poster serif). A font you uploaded yourself shows on the cover too — in the preview and in the downloaded files.
   say: Each block has its own font, colour and size. Three display faces ship with Beuk: Pinyon Script, a formal script; Patrick Hand, handwriting for children's books; and Abril Fatface, a heavy poster serif. A font you uploaded yourself shows on the cover too.
   do: show "Font"
3. Place a block with the **Position** sliders — across and down — or drag it on the cover itself. **Reset positions** puts the layout back in order when an experiment goes wrong.
   say: Place a block with the position sliders, or drag it on the cover itself. Reset positions puts the layout back in order when an experiment goes wrong.
   do: show "Reset positions"

## Takeaway
Type sells at thumbnail size: one face for the title, quieter type for everything else, and contrast between them. If a line fights the artwork, drag the block clear instead of shrinking the title.
