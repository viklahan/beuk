A reader meets the cover before the first sentence, usually at the size of a postage stamp. This course is five short lessons on Beuk's covers: the image that rides in the EPUB the way stores expect, the Cover Maker's type, the spine and the back of the printed wrap, and the files the stores and the printers ask for. Every lesson uses the sample novella, *The Lantern Coast*, so you can follow along on the same book, or on your own.
