The same manuscript that makes your ebook makes your paperback. The print settings live in Export too.

## Steps
1. Open **Export** and find the print section. Choose a trim size; each one says what kind of book it usually suits.
   say: Open Export, and scroll to the print section. Choose a trim size. Each one says what kind of book it usually suits, from a mass market paperback to a square children's book.
   do: click "Export"; wait 1; show "Trade paperback"
2. **Open Print View** to see the paginated pages as they will print.
   say: Open the print view to see the pages exactly as they will print, spread by spread.
   do: show "Open Print View"
3. Before you upload, **Run Print Preflight** checks the print file against the printer's rules.
   say: And before you upload, run print preflight. It checks the print file against the printer's rules, so a problem is caught here rather than at the printer.
   do: show "Run Print Preflight"

## Takeaway
One manuscript, two editions. Change a word once and both the ebook and the paperback have it.
