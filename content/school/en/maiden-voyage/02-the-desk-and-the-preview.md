The studio has two halves. You write on the page in the middle; the preview on the right shows the book as a reader will see it.

## Steps
1. Pick a chapter in the list on the left. It opens in **Chapter Content**, with its number, title and subtitle above the text.
   say: Pick a chapter on the left, and it opens in the middle, with its number, title and subtitle above the text.
   do: click "Ch. Two"
2. Switch the preview between reading devices: a Kindle Paperwhite, an iPad, a Kobo, a phone and more.
   say: The preview on the right can switch devices. Try an iPad, then a phone. What reads well on one should read well on all of them.
   do: click "Apple Books · iPad"; wait 1; click "Phone · OLED"; wait 1
3. Turn pages with **Next** and **Prev**, or click the preview and use the arrow keys.
   say: Turn the preview's pages with Next and Previous, or click into it and use the arrow keys.
   do: click "Kindle Paperwhite"; click "Next"; wait 1
4. Under the preview, the studio estimates how many pages the book runs on that device, section by section.
   say: Underneath, the studio estimates how many pages the book runs on that device, section by section.
   do: show "pages on Kindle Paperwhite"

## Takeaway
Write in the middle, read on the right: the preview is the book your reader will hold.
