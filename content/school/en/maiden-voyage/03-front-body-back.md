---
title: Front matter, body and back matter
blurb: The pages around your chapters, and how to add the ones your book needs.
---
A finished book is more than its chapters. The pages around them, from the half title to the colophon, are what make it look like a published book.

## Steps
1. **Front Matter** holds the pages before the story: the cover, half title, title page, copyright, epigraph, dedication and table of contents.
   say: Front matter holds the pages before the story starts: the cover, a half title, the title page, the copyright page, an epigraph, a dedication and the contents.
   do: click "Copyright"; wait 1
2. **Body** holds the story itself: a prologue, the chapters, and parts if your book has them.
   say: The body is the story itself: a prologue if you have one, then the chapters, and parts if the book is divided into them.
   do: show "Prologue"
3. **Back Matter** follows the story: acknowledgments, About the Author, Also by the Author and a colophon.
   say: Back matter follows the story: acknowledgments, about the author, a list of your other books, and a colophon.
   do: show "Back Matter"
4. **Add a page** offers the other kinds of page a book can carry, each in its proper place.
   say: Add a page lists the other kinds of page a book can carry, and puts each one in its proper place.
   do: click "Add a page"; wait 1; key Escape

## Takeaway
Readers notice when a book is missing its copyright page or its contents. The three tiers give each page a home.
