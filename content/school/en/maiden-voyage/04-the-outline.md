title: The outline board
blurb: Every section as a card: reorder the book, mark where each chapter stands, and filter by status.
---
The outline shows the whole book as cards. It is where you see the shape of the story and keep track of what still needs work.

## Steps
1. Open the **Outline** tab. Each card is a section of the book, with its words and scenes counted.
   say: Open the Outline tab. Each card is one section of the book, with its words and its scenes counted.
   do: tab "Outline"; wait 1
2. Drag a card to reorder the book, or open a section on the desk from its title.
   say: Drag a card to move that section, or open it on the desk from its title.
   do: show "Drag one to reorder the book"
3. Give each section a status, **To do**, **Draft**, **Revised** or **Final**, and filter the board by status or tag.
   say: Give each section a status: to do, draft, revised or final. Then filter the board to see just the sections that still need you.
   do: show "Revised"
4. Switch between **Cards** and **List**. Synopses, statuses and tags are notes for you: they are never exported or counted as words.
   say: Switch between cards and a list, whichever suits you. Synopses, statuses and tags are notes for you. They are never exported, and never counted as words.
   do: click "List"; wait 1; click "Cards"

## Takeaway
The outline is your map of the book. Nothing on it reaches a reader.
