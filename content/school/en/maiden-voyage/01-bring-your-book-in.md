Every book in Beuk starts the same way: on the desk. You can bring a manuscript in, start typing on the empty page, or open the sample story and look around first.

## Steps
1. Choose **Import Document** to bring in a Word (`.docx`), Markdown, plain-text, RTF (`.rtf`) or PDF file. A `.docx` keeps its headings, italics and blockquotes on the way in.
   say: To bring a manuscript in, choose Import Document. A Word file works best if you have one: its headings, italics and blockquotes come across with it.
   do: show "Import Document"
2. Not ready to import? **Load the sample story** puts a complete novella, *The Lantern Coast*, on the desk, at real book scale.
   say: If you just want to look around, load the sample story. It puts a complete novella on the desk, so every tool has a real book to work on.
   do: click "Load the sample story"; wait 1
3. The book is laid out in three tiers on the left: **Front Matter**, **Body** and **Back Matter**. Anything the importer placed wrong can be moved; nothing is locked.
   say: The book lands in three tiers on the left: front matter, the body, and back matter. If the importer put a section in the wrong place, you can move it. Nothing is locked.
   do: show "Front Matter"
4. Watch the save indicator in the header. The studio saves the whole book in this browser about a second after you pause.
   say: Up in the header, the save indicator tells you where the book is kept. The studio saves the whole book in this browser, about a second after you stop typing.
   do: show "Saved"

## Takeaway
Import, sample, or a blank page: whichever you choose, the book is saved on your device from the first word.
