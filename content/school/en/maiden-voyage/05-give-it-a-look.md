The Typography Studio sets the type for the whole book. Start from a theme, then change anything you like.

## Steps
1. Open **Typography Studio**. The theme presets cover fiction, nonfiction, memoir, academic, children's and poetry shelves.
   say: Open the Typography Studio. The themes at the top cover fiction, nonfiction, memoir, academic, children's books and poetry.
   do: tab "Typography Studio"; wait 1
2. Apply a theme in one click. It sets the fonts, rhythm, paragraph style and ornaments together, and the preview changes with it.
   say: One click applies a theme: the fonts, the rhythm of the lines, the paragraph style and the ornaments, all together. Watch the preview change.
   do: click "Noir & Thriller"; wait 2
3. Try another to compare. Every value a theme sets stays adjustable below it.
   say: Try another to compare. Nothing is fixed: every value a theme sets can still be adjusted further down.
   do: click "Classic Literary"; wait 2

## Takeaway
Pick the theme that suits the book's shelf first. Fine-tune later, if at all.
