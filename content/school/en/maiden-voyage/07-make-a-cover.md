Every book needs a face. If you have artwork, upload it. If not, the Cover Maker builds one from your book's own details.

## Steps
1. Open **Project Settings**. The cover block is where you upload a cover, or make one.
   say: Open Project Settings. The cover block is where you upload artwork, or make a cover from scratch.
   do: click "Project Settings"; wait 1
2. Choose **Make a Cover**. The title, subtitle and author are already filled in from your book.
   say: Choose Make a Cover. The title, subtitle and author are already filled in from your book.
   do: click "Make a Cover"; wait 2
3. Try the layouts: classic centred, modern stacked, editorial, banded and more. Drag any text block on the cover to place it exactly.
   say: Try the layouts. Each one arranges the same words differently, and you can drag any block of text to put it exactly where you want.
   do: click "Banded"; wait 1; click "Classic Centred"; wait 1
4. Pick a background: a solid colour, a gradient, a texture, your own art, a duotone or a photo.
   say: Then pick a background: a solid colour, a gradient, a texture, your own art, a duotone, or a photograph.
   do: click "Gradient"; wait 1; click "My Art"; wait 1

## Takeaway
A cover made here follows the book: change the title later and the cover changes with it.
