Story Insights reads your manuscript and shows its shape: how long it is, how the chapters compare, and how much of it is dialogue. Everything is counted on your device.

## Steps
1. Open **Insights** at the foot of the sidebar. **At a glance** shows words, chapters, reading time, pages, average sentence length and dialogue share.
   say: Open Insights, at the foot of the sidebar. At a glance shows the words, chapters, reading time, pages, average sentence length, and how much of the book is dialogue.
   do: click "Insights"; wait 2
2. **The words that carry the book** lists your most-used words, with the small filler words set aside.
   say: Below that, the words that carry the book: your most used words, with the little filler words set aside.
   do: show "The words that carry the book"
3. **Chapter rhythm** charts every chapter's length against the book's average. Choose a chapter to open it.
   say: Chapter rhythm charts each chapter's length against the average, so a chapter that runs long or short stands out. Choose one to open it.
   do: show "Chapter rhythm"

## Takeaway
The numbers describe the draft. What to do with them is still your call.
