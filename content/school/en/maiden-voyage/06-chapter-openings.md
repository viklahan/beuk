The first lines of a chapter are where a printed book shows its craft. Each chapter in Beuk carries its own opening settings.

## Steps
1. On **Chapter Content**, a **Drop cap** opens the chapter with a large initial letter.
   say: On the chapter's page, the drop cap switch opens the chapter with a large initial letter.
   do: tab "Chapter Content"; show "Drop cap"
2. A **Small-caps lead-in** sets the first words of the chapter in small capitals.
   say: A small caps lead-in sets the first few words in small capitals, the traditional way into a chapter.
   do: show "Small-caps lead-in"
3. An **Opening plate** puts a full-bleed illustration on its own page just before the chapter, and **Plate alt text** describes it for readers who cannot see it.
   say: An opening plate puts a full page illustration just before the chapter. Give it alt text, so readers who can't see the picture still know what it shows.
   do: show "Opening plate"
4. A **Chapter backdrop** lays art behind the chapter's opening, beneath the words, at the strength you choose.
   say: A chapter backdrop lays art behind the opening, beneath the words, as faint or as strong as you like.
   do: show "Chapter backdrop"

## Takeaway
Openings are set per chapter, so a prologue can look different from chapter one.
