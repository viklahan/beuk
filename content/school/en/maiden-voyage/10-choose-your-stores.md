When the book is ready, Export makes the files. One manuscript becomes an EPUB for each store you sell in.

## Steps
1. Open **Export** in the header.
   say: Open Export, at the top right.
   do: click "Export"; wait 1
2. Under **EPUB 3.2 — Platform Presets**, pick where the book is going: Amazon KDP, Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play, Draft2Digital and more.
   say: Under the platform presets, pick where the book is going: Amazon, Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play, an aggregator like Draft2Digital, and others.
   do: show "Platform Presets"; click "Amazon KDP"; wait 1
3. Read the preset's notes. They say exactly what changes in the package for that store, and what the file will be called.
   say: Read the notes under the presets. They say exactly what changes in the file for that store, and what the file will be called.
   do: click "Apple Books"; wait 1
4. **Generic EPUB** is the original package with nothing store-specific added: right for direct sales, libraries and sideloading.
   say: Generic EPUB is the original package, with nothing store specific added. It's the right choice for selling direct, for libraries, and for sideloading.
   do: click "Generic EPUB"; wait 1

## Takeaway
Pick the preset for the store you'll upload to. Each one builds from the same manuscript.
