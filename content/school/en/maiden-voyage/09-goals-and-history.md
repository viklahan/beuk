Two tools sit beside Insights at the foot of the sidebar: Goals, for keeping the work moving, and History, for going back in time.

## Steps
1. Open **Goals** to set a daily target, a target for the whole book or a deadline.
   say: Goals is where you set a daily target, a length for the whole book, or a deadline.
   do: click "Goals"; wait 2; key Escape
2. Open **History** to see the snapshots the studio keeps of your book, and bring back an earlier version.
   say: History keeps snapshots of the book as you work, so you can go back to an earlier version if you need to.
   do: click "History"; wait 2; key Escape

## Takeaway
Set a goal you can keep, and write without fear: History has the earlier versions.
