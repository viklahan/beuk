What you're doing: nothing, mostly. The streak simply notices the days you show up for your book — and it's built, deliberately, to forgive the days you don't.

## Explanation

### How it works

- When you're signed in and active in the studio, the server stamps *its own* date for the day — the streak can't be gamed by a clock, and can't be lost to one either.
- Your panel shows the current streak, your longest, how many **freezes** you hold, and whether you're currently paused.
- New accounts start with one freeze in hand. The streak is a shelf you build, not a chain you're dragging.

### The forgiving parts

- **Repair.** Miss a day, come back, and the gap is quietly covered by a freeze if you have one — the streak survives, and the ledger shows honestly that a repair happened.
- **Pause.** Going away? Plan it: a pause pre-covers up to 30 days, costs no freezes, and your panel says you're paused so future-you isn't greeted by an accusation.
- **No guilt mechanics.** No shaming notifications, no public leaderboards, no broken-chain theatre. A streak that restarts simply restarts — your longest is remembered, and the book is still there.

## Troubleshooting

### What can go wrong

**I wrote today but the streak didn't move.** The day is stamped by activity while signed in. If you worked offline or signed out, the day wasn't recorded —[[if-support]] write to us if it matters and we'll look;[[/if-support]] but know the streak is a nudge, not a grade.

**My streak restarted.** That happens when a gap is longer than your freezes could cover. It stings for a second; it's also just true. Your longest streak is kept, and day one of the new one is today.
