The beacon has been answered. The map points north. And something under a black tower is listening.

Zib and Pip are going north — across the Howling Wastes, where the wind plays the stone like an instrument and the Whisperwings hunt by sound. Somewhere past the canyons, past the Warden patrols, past everything the maps admit to, stands the Spire: a needle of black stone holding up the sky, with a name nobody says aloud.

They're not the only ones heading that way. Scrapjaw wants the beacon core, and Scrapjaw always collects. And the farther north they go, the stranger the song gets.

> Come down, little ships. Come down where it's warm.
