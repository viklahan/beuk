Each picture is a puzzle, and your eyes are the trick.

Ten faces, each one made of two animals mashed together: name both. Three tricks your eyes play on you, the dress among them (on the dress there are no wrong answers, only arguments). Three scenes with animals camouflaged into a forest, a reef and a canyon: count them and name them.

39 points in all, from "Beautifully wrong — but you had fun, which was the point" up to Eagle eyes. Every answer is at the back.

> Check the answers at the back — no peeking.
