The weather turned on the first of September, and it was not weather anyone could see.

Korean Air Lines Flight 007 has been shot down over Sakhalin, every indicator board in Moscow is lit, and Babeetza Romanov has learned that the fabricated indicators feeding both capitals' fear did not just pass through Moscow Station. They were written there. Now the dead-drop chain she spent five months building westward has to turn around and point east, at her own building.

The hunt runs from Moscow to Helsinki, West and East Berlin, and Mons, in Belgium, as the Able Archer exercise begins in November 1983.

> Western analysts cited Soviet fear as evidence; Soviet analysts cited Western reporting as confirmation; each capital photocopied the other's terror until the copy became the original. The mole hunt in this book is fiction. The mirrors were real.

Thirty chapters in five parts, a tradecraft glossary, a cast list, a sneak peek at Volume Three, and fifteen full-page illustrations.
