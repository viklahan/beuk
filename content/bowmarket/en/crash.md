Zib had exactly three thoughts as his ship fell out of the sky: this is fine; this is fine?; THIS IS NOT FINE.

The Lucky Break comes down on Vex, a small violet world in permanent dusk, with two moons, a desert of black glass that sings, and a market in the belly of a crashed freighter where everything is for sale and nothing is refunded. Zib, certified pilot (he has a badge), is not alone for long: Pip, a moonling who speaks only in squeaks, arrives in the second chapter and, in the author's words, immediately takes over the book.

There is a beacon that blinks, a Warden's price to pay, a Great Glass Race, and at the edge of the world a smudge of black against the sky where no mountain ought to be.

> This book was written to be read aloud. The squeaks especially. Do not be shy about them.

Twelve chapters, a traveler's guide to Vex, a glossary of squeaks, and five full-page illustrations.
