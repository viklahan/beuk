Dawn in Nassau roads, October 1717, and Mercy Fell musters the crew of the Carrion: fourteen guns, ninety souls, and the Articles read aloud so that no man hangs for breaking what he never heard.

Inside her coat she carries an account-book, begun at Port Royal in 1714. Three names in it are paid. The last is not: Nathaniel Rook, captain, who sold seventy men for a commission.

> Not for king and not for country. For the share.

Thirty chapters, from the muster in Nassau roads to the last run into the storm, with fifteen plates of ships, gunsmoke and weather.
