First day of fifth grade in Room 204, and Milo Puddle has made a vow: this year he is going to be NORMAL. No weird incidents. No cafeteria disasters.

Then Principal Okafor announces that Old Reliable, the cafeteria's nacho cheese pump, the pump that has never once clogged, is being retired at semester's end. Milo plans it a farewell: the legendary double-pump, performed with love and respect for the valve. On Nacho Friday the valve sticks, a geyser of cheese hits the ceiling, and nobody sees who did it. By lunch on Monday the school paper has a name for the culprit: the Cheese Bandit.

Only Theo knows the truth. Then Mason Crisp, who has perfect hair and is running for class president, turns up with Milo's lost retainer in a ziplock bag labelled EVIDENCE.

> Old Reliable is dead. I killed it. With love. Which is the worst way to kill anything.

Four months of diary entries, September to December, with 23 illustrations.
